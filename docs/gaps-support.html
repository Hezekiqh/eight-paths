Gaps support

Need help with Gaps, found a bug or have an idea? Email hezekiahhopkins@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

Common questions

What is the average gap?
The average time between your last five slips. Once your current gap is longer than the average, it starts counting toward it, so you can watch the average climb live.
I logged a slip by mistake.
Tap Undo within 10 seconds. After that, open Stats, find it in the slip log and tap it to change the time or delete it.
How do I change the timer's units?
Tap the timer. It cycles through the clock, seconds, minutes, hours, days, weeks, months, years and all units at once.
Notifications aren't arriving.
Check Settings → Notifications in Gaps, then iOS Settings → Notifications → Gaps. Messages only arrive inside the hours you set, and the app refills its schedule each time you open it.
Where is my data?
Only on your phone. There is no account. Settings → Your data lets you export a CSV or delete everything.

If you're struggling

Gaps is a tracking tool, not medical advice or a treatment service. In the US, the SAMHSA National Helpline is free, confidential and open 24/7 at 1-800-662-4357. You can call or text 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. If you are in immediate danger, call 911.
