Gaps privacy policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Gaps tracks the time between slips for habits you want to do less. What you track is personal, so Gaps is built to keep it on your phone.

What Gaps collects

Nothing. Gaps has no account, no sign-in and no server. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

What stays on your phone

The habits you add, the times of your slips, the causes and notes you write, your action plans, your mantra and your settings are saved only in the app's storage on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else.

  • Export: Settings → Your data → Export slips creates a CSV file that you choose where to send. Nothing is exported unless you do it.
  • Delete: Settings → Your data → Delete all data erases everything immediately. Deleting the app does the same.
  • Backups: if you use iCloud or computer backups for your iPhone, Apple may include the app's data in those backups, under Apple's own privacy terms.

Notifications

If you turn on motivation messages, they are scheduled on your phone by the app itself. They never name a habit or a cause, and you can hide the message text from the lock screen in Settings.

Children

Gaps is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
