Pocket Brain privacy policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Pocket Brain helps you remember people, thoughts and to-dos by voice. What you tell it is personal, so it is built to keep everything on your iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. Pocket Brain has no account, no sign-in and no server. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code. We never see your names, thoughts, to-dos or recordings.

What stays on your phone

The people you save (names, notes, what they look like, promises, memories), your thoughts and feelings, your to-dos, the places where you met people, and your voice recordings are stored only in the app on your device.

  • Export: Settings → Export creates a file that you choose where to send. Nothing is exported unless you do it.
  • Delete: Settings → Delete everything erases all of it immediately. Deleting the app does the same.
  • Backups: if you use iCloud or computer backups for your iPhone, Apple may include the app's data in those backups, under Apple's own privacy terms.

Your voice

When you talk to a mic button, Pocket Brain records audio and turns it into text with Apple's speech recognition. On iPhones that support it, this happens on your device. On some older devices, Apple may process the audio on its servers under Apple's privacy policy. Recordings are kept in the app so you can play them back, and are deleted when you delete the item.

Summaries (Apple Intelligence)

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, summaries, feelings, names and to-do details are worked out by Apple's on-device model. Nothing is sent to us or to any other AI service.

Location

If you allow it, Pocket Brain saves where you were when you saved a name, so it can show you the people you know at a place. Place searches use Apple Maps. Your locations are stored only on your device.

Face ID

If you turn on the app lock, iOS checks your face or passcode. The app never sees or stores that data.

Children

Pocket Brain is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
