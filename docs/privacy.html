Privacy Policy

Effective 26 September 2026

In short: Eight Paths does not collect, store or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores

To run the game, Eight Paths saves your player name, chosen class, quests, completed quests, rest tokens and reminder time. This information is saved only in the app's private storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account or sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash reporting, and it does not make network requests with your data.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a single evening reminder on your device. These are local notifications created and delivered by iOS; no data is sent to a server to deliver them. You can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your iPhone permanently removes all of its data. If your device backups include app data, a copy may remain in those backups under your control.

Children

Eight Paths does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.

Health information

Eight Paths is a habit game. It is not a medical, clinical or mental health service and does not provide medical advice. The app links to the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (US); contacting 988 is between you and the Lifeline and is covered by their privacy practices.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
