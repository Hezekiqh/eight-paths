Pocket Brain support

Need help with Pocket Brain, found a bug or have an idea? Email hezekiahhopkins@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

Common questions

How do I save something by voice?
Each tab has its own mic. Hold it and talk, or tap once to start and tap again to stop. Double-tap the mic, or tap the small keyboard, to type instead.
The mic doesn't hear me.
Open iOS Settings → Pocket Brain and turn on Microphone and Speech Recognition. Settings in the app shows whether voice is ready.
Why are there no summaries?
Summaries and feeling guesses use Apple Intelligence, which needs a supported iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on. Without it, Pocket Brain still saves everything you say, word for word.
How does it know where I met someone?
If you allow location, the place is saved with the name. You can also search for a place on a person's page, or tap "Use where I am now".
Where is my data?
Only on your phone. There is no account. Settings lets you export everything or delete everything.

If you're struggling

Pocket Brain is a memory helper, not medical or mental health advice. In the US, you can call or text 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. If you are in immediate danger, call 911.
