Common questions

How do I undo a completed quest?
Tap it again on the Today tab. Undo works for the same day only.
Why did a class fade on my radar?
Classes you haven't completed a quest for in 3 days dim, and after 7 days they dim further. One completed quest brings them back to full brightness. Levels and XP are never lost.
What are rest tokens?
Miss a habit and a rest token is used automatically at midnight to save that habit's streak: one token for each habit missed. Tokens don't save the day itself, so your days-in-a-row streak still resets on a day with nothing done. You start with one, earn another for every 7 days in a row that you play, and can hold up to three.
How do I change or turn off the evening reminder?
Change the time in Character → Settings → Evening reminder. To turn reminders off, go to iOS Settings → Notifications → Eight Paths.
How do I start over?
Delete the app and reinstall it. All progress is stored only on your device, so this cannot be undone.

If you're struggling

Eight Paths is a habit game, not a mental health service. In the US, you can call or text 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, free and confidential, 24/7. If you are in immediate danger, call 911.
