# Season 1: more world

Oct 7, 2026 · a plan for the author to approve before anything is built. Canon lives in STORY.md; this file is
what we build, and where. Nothing here is in the game yet.

The aim, in the author's words: more places to reveal lore, more places for fights, more places for puzzles, and more
places for party banter. Every new place below does at least three of the four.

## New places

### 1. The Cull Road (the way to Old Morrow's garden)

Old Morrow's garden at the deserters' camp is too easy to reach today: Warrior City's road north (Lv 10), then
straight in, with no fights. The Cull Road goes in between.

- **Where:** Warrior City → the Cull Road → the deserters' camp. The road the unfit children are walked down at 12.
- **Fights:** tithe-takers (raiders) who still patrol it for runaways, then shadows at the camp's broken gate.
- **Puzzle:** a washed-out bridge. Push boulders onto the plates to raise the old ferry.
- **Lore (Told):** milestones carved by the children ("I was 12. I was not strong enough."), and a tithe-taker's
  orders: who goes to the Yard, who goes to the camp.
- **Banter:** Pip and Moss on the children, Brannoc unusually quiet.

### 2. The Graveyard of Kings (beside the chapel, above the crypt)

- **Three tombstones**, word for word:
  - THE MAD KING, OR SO THEY SAY. REST IN PEACE.
  - THE BEAUTIFUL QUEEN, OR SO THEY SAY. REST IN PEACE.
  - THE LOST PRINCE, OR SO THEY SAY. REST IN PEACE.

  Each has a few small lines underneath on who they were, in what people believe.
- **Lore (Hinted):** rows of soldiers' graves, **empty, and dug from the inside**. One big grave with a broken chain:
  THE WARDEN. Kaldor's power over the dead, without anyone saying so.
- **Fights:** the risen dead (shadow soldiers), at the empty graves.
- **Puzzle:** match the epitaphs to the graves (or light the lanterns in order) to open the crypt gate. This replaces
  the Cleric job as the way into the crypt, or sits beside it.
- **Banter:** Brannoc at his own grave. Wren on the dead who won't stay down.

### 3. The Royal Forest (where Brannoc ran)

- **Where:** south of Warrior City, past the Royal Garden on the south road.
- **Lore (Told):** Brannoc's drawings, carved and scratched into the trees along the path he ran, getting wilder the
  deeper you go. At the heart, a ring of dead grass where the shadows closed round him, and the broken husk of an old
  cocoon. With Brannoc in the party, a memory: shadows on every side, and that's all he remembers.
- **Fights:** shadows, thickening towards the heart.
- **Puzzle:** follow his drawings. Each one points the way he ran; the forest loops back on anyone who doesn't.
- **Banter:** Brannoc remembers bits; Moss reads the trees.

### 4. The Painters' School (ruins, Kingdom Town's old quarter)

- **Lore (Told):** Brannoc's teacher, the painter and philosopher. A statue, his unfinished canvases, and a student's
  sketchbook (B's).
- **Puzzle:** hang the fallen paintings back in order. They tell the story of a boy and a sword; done right, they play
  **Brannoc's flashback** (the memory system, like the schoolyard): the confession, "yearning for battle doesn't make one
  a great warrior", "the responsibilities of the crown are heavy", fade to black, the school closed, the teacher nearly
  dead.
- **Fights:** none. It's a quiet place on purpose.
- **Banter:** Brannoc, and whoever is with him, after the flashback. This is where his dream is told in full (below).

### 5. The Bank of Warrior City (Ysolde's, so the Noble has something to say)

- **Lore (Told):** the ledgers. Rations by a family's child's strength, champions' stipends, mothers of champions paid
  like goddesses, and **SPOILS OF THE ISLAND. 500 SOLDIERS. ONE DAY.** The war chest, very full.
- **Puzzle:** the vault. Its combination is in the ledgers, if you read them as a banker would (Ysolde, or a Noble of
  the right level, spots it).
- **Fights:** optional, the vault's guards, if you open it without the combination.
- **Banter:** Ysolde has opinions on every ledger.

## Across the game

### Ask a hero about their dream

In each hero's room off the Archive: **"What was your dream?"**

- **Not their time yet:** a little, not much (one line, a deflection).
- **During their arc:** the whole dream. In Season 1 that's Brannoc, after the Painters' School: he wanted to paint, and
  to be free.

The other seven need dreams written: one teaser line each now, the full dream saved for their arc.

### Party banter while walking

Today banter only plays after you talk to someone. Add **walking banter**: party members talk among themselves when you
enter a place or pass a spot (once each), so personalities come through between conversations. Every new place gets
some, and so do existing ones: the Kaloseum stands, Kaldorhold's gate, the deserters' camp, the crypt.

### More fights

Beyond the new places, the plan is to add patrols to existing roads that have none: the Courier Road after Felix, the
March Road, the tithe road.

### The ending, as STORY.md now has it

- **Brannoc asks "What do we do?"** after you beat Kaldor.
- **Kind or mean variants:** your answer depends on your choice and on your record of kind and mean answers. Honor is
  counted already, but nothing reads it yet.
- **Your name:** Brannoc says it ("OK, *name*. As you command."). It's read from onboarding.
- **Felix:** "This is turning out better than I expected." Then he fades into nothing.

Make sure the Warden scene looks good. Images of it are being made now, and fixes follow.
